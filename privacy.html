This Privacy Policy describes how TikTok Auto Reply Bot ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you connect your TikTok account to our service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide the automated reply service:

  • TikTok OAuth Access Token: Obtained through TikTok's official OAuth 2.0 login flow, used to authenticate API requests on your behalf.
  • Basic Account Information: Your TikTok display name and open ID (a non-reversible identifier), used solely to identify your account within the dashboard.
  • Comment Data (Transient): Comments on your TikTok videos are fetched in real time to evaluate keyword rules. Comment content is not stored permanently — it is processed in memory and discarded after rule evaluation.
  • Bot Configuration: Keyword rules and reply templates you create within the App are stored to power the automation.

We do not collect passwords, payment information, or any data unrelated to the automated reply function.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your TikTok account and execute API calls on your behalf.
  • To monitor comments on your videos and apply your configured keyword-trigger rules.
  • To post automated replies using the messages you have pre-approved.
  • To display your account name in the App dashboard for identification.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the service you have requested.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your data with any third party, except:

  • TikTok API: All automated replies and data fetches are made through TikTok's official API and are subject to TikTok's own privacy policy.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or valid legal process.

4. Data Retention

Access tokens are stored in encrypted form and are deleted immediately upon account disconnection or token revocation. Comment data is never written to persistent storage — it exists only transiently during processing. Bot configuration (keyword rules and reply templates) is retained until you delete it or terminate your account.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption at rest and in transit (TLS 1.2+), access control, and regular security reviews. While we strive to protect your information, no system can guarantee absolute security over the internet.

6. TikTok API Data Policy Compliance

This application is built on the TikTok for Developers API and complies with TikTok's API Terms of Service and Data Policy. We use only the scopes necessary for the described functionality and handle all TikTok user data in accordance with TikTok's developer guidelines.

7. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Revoke the App's access to your TikTok account via your TikTok account settings.
  • Request deletion of all data we hold about you by contacting us at the email below.
  • Update or delete your bot configuration rules from within the App dashboard.

8. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
